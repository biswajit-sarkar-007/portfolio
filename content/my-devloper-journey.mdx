I started my coding journey and learning technology in class 12 (2024). It has been two years, and although my learning hasn’t been in a structured way, I’ve been driven by curiosity, exploring many things—and I’m loving it.

Then came my college entry era in the first year (mid 2024–25). At that time, I felt totally lost, but somehow I managed to focus and restart my learning. I wasn’t bad at it, and now, as I’m finishing my 3rd year (2026), I’m trying to accelerate my learning just like I did before.

Currently, I am working as a part-time employee at a startup company as a fresher. They pay me a decent amount, and I see this as an opportunity to learn more, build more, and make more connections.
